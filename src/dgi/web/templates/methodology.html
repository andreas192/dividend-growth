Methodology

Companies are scored from 0 to 100 on four pillars. Each metric is mapped to 0–100 with the bands below (absolute bands, so a score does not shift when the universe changes), then averaged inside its pillar by weight; the total is the weighted mean of the pillars a company has data for. Missing metrics lower the company's coverage; below {{ (cfg.universe.min_coverage * 100)|round(0)|int }}% coverage it is not ranked. These values come from config/scoring.yaml.

Screener filters (defaults)

  • Minimum years of dividend increases: {{ cfg.hard_filters.min_streak }}
  • Maximum payout of free cash flow: {{ cfg.hard_filters.max_payout_fcf|pct(0) }}
  • Minimum market cap: {{ cfg.hard_filters.min_market_cap|big }}
  • Filters are applied separately from the score and can be changed on the screener.

Who is scored

  • At least {{ cfg.universe.min_dividend_years }} complete years of dividend history, a recent price and fundamentals from the last {{ cfg.universe.max_fundamentals_age_days }} days.
  • Banks, insurers and REITs are listed as "not scored" (SIC ranges {% for lo, hi in cfg.universe.excluded_sic_ranges %}{{ lo }}–{{ hi }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}): payout and coverage ratios do not describe those businesses.

Dividend handling

  • Payments are restated to today's share basis using split events before they are summed, so splits never create fake cuts or raises.
  • Annual dividends are calendar-year sums of complete years. A raise is more than {{ (cfg.metrics.raise_tolerance * 100)|round(1) }}% above the prior year, a cut more than {{ (cfg.metrics.cut_tolerance * 100)|round(1) }}% below it.
  • An extra payment at least {{ cfg.metrics.special_ratio }}× the median regular one, in a year with more payments than usual, is a special dividend: excluded from the totals and flagged.
  • Payout ratios use cash dividends paid over net income and over free cash flow, shown up to {{ (cfg.metrics.payout_cap * 100)|round(0)|int }}%. When a filer reports no dividends paid, the year's regular dividend per share times diluted shares stands in.
  • The streak counts back to the start of the available dividend history (usually around 1970). A plus sign after it means the real record may be longer.

Fair value

Gordon growth on the trailing dividend: value = dividend × (1 + g) / (r − g), with required return r = {{ (cfg.valuation.required_return * 100)|round(1) }}% and growth g equal to the 5-year dividend growth, kept between {{ (cfg.valuation.growth_floor * 100)|round(0)|int }}% and {{ (cfg.valuation.growth_cap * 100)|round(0)|int }}%. The range moves r and g by {{ (cfg.valuation.range_delta * 100)|round(1) }} points. It is a sensitivity aid, not a price target.

Warnings

Shown outside the score: a dividend cut or suspension in the last five years; a payout above 100% of earnings or of free cash flow; negative free cash flow; a dividend in the price data at or above the share price (likely a data error); and, as notes, special dividends and an irregular number of payments.
